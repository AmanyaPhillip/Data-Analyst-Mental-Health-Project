o	"Regional Concentration".
•	•  Geographic Distribution: ON stands for Ontario and QC stands for Quebec, representing Canada's two most populous provinces.
•	•  Population Concentration: The statement "60%+ in ON/QC" means that more than 60% of the total volume of distressed individuals, mental health service strain, or population-wide clinical impact across the country is concentrated within these two provinces alone.
•	•  Executive Significance: This highlights for decision-makers that while smaller provinces like Nova Scotia experience a higher percentage rate of burden per capita, the absolute majority of national distress volume lives in Ontario and Quebec due to their sheer population size.
